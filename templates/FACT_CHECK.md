## Final pass

- [ ] Every material factual claim has appropriate support or is clearly framed as opinion/inference
- [ ] No citation was generated merely to fill a gap
- [ ] DOI/PMID/author/title/year or issuing body verified where used
- [ ] Important clinical numbers checked against underlying sources
- [ ] Quotations are exact, minimal, and attributable
- [ ] Evidence certainty is not overstated
- [ ] Conflicting or materially limiting evidence is represented
- [ ] FDA-approved, off-label, investigational, compounded, or unapproved status is distinguished when relevant
- [ ] Jurisdiction and NP scope-of-practice context is current when relevant
- [ ] No PHI or unnecessary sensitive information appears
- [ ] Final diff reviewed for accidental truncation or omitted sections
