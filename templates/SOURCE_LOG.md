## Rules

- Do not cite a source as evidence when `Inspection status` is only `discovered`.
- Never invent missing DOI, PMID, page, author, date, or bibliographic fields.
- Preserve enough source identity to allow another reviewer to re-find it.
- For connected Drive sources, reference the source identifier/link without copying unnecessary copyrighted or sensitive content into the repo.
- Record meaningful conflicts, corrections, retractions, and outdated guidance when found.
