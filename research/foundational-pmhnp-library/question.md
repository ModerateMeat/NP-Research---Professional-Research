# Research Question: Foundational PMHNP connected-source inventory

## Primary question

What high-value foundational PMHNP references are already available in the connected Google Drive, what role should each source play in future research/writing, and what verification or provenance limits apply before publication-facing use?

## Why this question matters

The repository is intended to function as a reproducible research-and-writing harness. Before topic-specific work begins, the project needs a clear map of useful connected references and an explicit boundary between orientation material and evidence suitable for final clinical or professional claims.

## Structured frame

- **Population / problem:** PMHNP professional research and psychiatric clinical-writing topics.
- **Intervention / exposure:** Not applicable; this is a source-inventory question.
- **Comparator:** Not applicable.
- **Outcomes:** Re-findable source inventory, source-role classification, duplicate detection, provenance/use boundaries, and verification requirements.
- **Setting / context:** Private GitHub research harness with connected Google Drive source material.
- **Time horizon:** Inventory reviewed on 2026-09-03; current clinical/legal claims require fresh verification at the time of use.

## Decision boundaries

### Meaningful support
A source is useful for the inventory when it is relevant to a recurring PMHNP research domain and enough of the source or metadata has been inspected to classify its role without guessing.

### Uncertainty
If bibliographic details, provenance, edition currency, or content relevance have not been fully verified, the source remains `partially inspected` and is not treated as final publication authority.

### Contradiction
If a reference text conflicts with a newer guideline, regulator, official labeling, statute, board rule, or stronger/current evidence, the newer authoritative source governs the publication-facing claim.

### Insufficient evidence
A textbook/reference source alone is insufficient for a time-sensitive treatment, dosing, safety, regulatory, epidemiologic, or scope-of-practice claim when a more current authoritative source is available or required.

## Scope

### Include

- PMHNP role/scope/certification references.
- Evidence-based-practice methodology.
- Advanced-practice psychiatric psychotherapy references.
- Psychopharmacology/neuroscience references.
- Child/adolescent psychiatric assessment references.
- Duplicate detection.
- Source provenance and publication-use boundaries.

### Exclude

- Topic-specific treatment recommendations.
- Copying textbook chapters, tables, images, or large excerpts.
- Patient-specific information or PHI.
- Assuming that material stored in Drive has publisher-authorized provenance.
- Treating reference texts as current regulatory or legal authority.

## Evidence priorities

- **Primary studies needed:** Later, for topic-specific efficacy, safety, psychometrics, mechanisms, or outcomes claims.
- **Systematic reviews/meta-analyses needed:** Later, for synthesis-level clinical claims and comparative effectiveness.
- **Guidelines/consensus statements needed:** Later, for current practice recommendations.
- **Regulatory/official sources needed:** FDA labeling/safety information; ANCC/credentialing information; Texas Board of Nursing and applicable statutes/rules for jurisdictional questions; other official agencies as relevant.
- **Connected Drive sources to inspect first:** Zakhari PMHNP review; Melnyk EBP reference; Wheeler psychotherapy reference; Stahl psychopharmacology reference; Youngstrom/Prinstein/Mash child-adolescent assessment reference.

## Recency requirements

- **Search/review date:** 2026-09-03.
- **Earliest publication date:** No fixed cutoff for foundational orientation sources.
- **Topics requiring current verification:** dosing, boxed warnings, contraindications, FDA approval status, treatment recommendations, epidemiology, screening recommendations, certification rules, NP scope-of-practice, Texas law/regulation, and any claim described as current/latest.

## Safety / interpretation notes

- **FDA-approved vs off-label/investigational distinctions:** Must be checked against current official labeling and evidence when relevant.
- **Population limitations:** Pediatric, geriatric, pregnancy/lactation, substance-use, medical-comorbidity, and other special-population claims require source-specific verification.
- **Contraindications/monitoring:** Must be verified from current authoritative sources for medication or treatment claims.
- **Jurisdiction/scope-of-practice:** Current Texas BON/statutory/regulatory sources govern Texas-specific claims; textbooks are background only.
