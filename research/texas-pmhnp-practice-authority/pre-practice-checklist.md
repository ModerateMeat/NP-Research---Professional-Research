# Texas PMHNP Pre-Practice / Onboarding Checklist

**Use:** before first patient care in a new Texas PMHNP role, and again when the practice site, delegating physician, prescribing scope, or telemedicine footprint changes  
**Research review date:** September 3, 2026  
**Parent sprint:** #7

> This is an implementation checklist derived from the detailed research in this folder. It is not individualized legal advice. If an item cannot be verified, treat it as a **stop item** rather than assuming the employer has handled it.

## Gate 1 — RN + APRN credentials

Do not begin PMHNP practice until all applicable credential layers are actually active.

- [ ] I have a valid RN privilege to practice in Texas.
- [ ] My graduate APRN education is congruent with the PMHNP role/population focus.
- [ ] My current national PMHNP certification is recognized for Texas licensure.
- [ ] My **Texas BON APRN license in the PMHNP role/population focus has been issued**; I am not relying on graduation or national certification alone.
- [ ] I am using APRN/PMHNP titles and credentials in a manner consistent with my issued Texas license.
- [ ] The patient population and services expected in this job fit the PMHNP role/population focus.
- [ ] I have the individual education, training, and demonstrated competence for the specific services expected.

**Detailed reference:** [`licensure-scope.md`](licensure-scope.md)

### STOP if

- Texas APRN/PMHNP licensure is still pending;
- the job expects another APRN role/population focus that I do not hold; or
- the employer is treating physician delegation or on-the-job training as a substitute for required APRN role/population-focus education/licensure.

---

## Gate 2 — Employer credentialing + privileges

Legal/professional scope and employer permission are separate questions.

- [ ] Employer credentialing is complete.
- [ ] Required clinical privileges are approved in writing where applicable.
- [ ] The job description accurately reflects PMHNP scope and expected population.
- [ ] Required competencies/orientation are documented.
- [ ] Malpractice/professional liability coverage is confirmed for the actual services and practice sites.
- [ ] Employer policies do not assign services outside my individual or licensed PMHNP scope.

### STOP if

The employer says an activity is permitted merely because “the physician will supervise/delegate it” when the activity is outside the PMHNP role/population focus or my individual competence.

---

## Gate 3 — Written physician delegation for medical aspects of care

Texas medical delegation is a separate layer from PMHNP licensure.

- [ ] A Texas-licensed physician relationship is established for the delegated medical aspects of care required by the role.
- [ ] The written delegation protocol/practice agreement is signed by the applicable parties.
- [ ] The agreement actually covers the medical acts expected in this position (for example diagnosis/medical management, diagnostic testing, medical plans of care, or prescribing as applicable).
- [ ] The document is current and scheduled for at least annual review/re-signature.
- [ ] The agreement is maintained where required and can be produced if requested.
- [ ] I know how and when the delegating physician/alternate physicians are available for consultation/referral/emergencies.

**Detailed reference:** [`delegation-prescriptive-authority.md`](delegation-prescriptive-authority.md)

### STOP if

The practice involves medical aspects of care but nobody can identify a current written delegation mechanism that covers those acts.

---

## Gate 4 — Prescriptive authority

Complete this gate before ordering or prescribing drugs/devices.

- [ ] My Texas prescriptive authority/prescription authorization is active.
- [ ] Physician-delegated prescriptive authority is in place.
- [ ] The practice setting has been correctly classified as non-facility-based vs qualifying hospital/LTC facility-based practice.
- [ ] A valid PAA or qualifying facility-based protocol/other written authorization is in place for this setting.
- [ ] The document identifies the drug/device categories I am expected to prescribe.
- [ ] Consultation/referral and patient-emergency plans are defined.
- [ ] The quality-assurance process is defined.
- [ ] Required **monthly QA meetings** are operational when the PAA pathway applies.
- [ ] The physician chart-review process is defined and documented.
- [ ] The agreement is reviewed/signed at least annually.
- [ ] The prescriptive-delegation relationship is correctly registered through the Texas Medical Board process.
- [ ] The practice has a method to retain terminated PAAs for the required period.

**Detailed reference:** [`delegation-prescriptive-authority.md`](delegation-prescriptive-authority.md)

### STOP if

- the employer calls a freestanding clinic “hospital based” without verifying that it qualifies for the facility-based statutory/rule pathway;
- the practice cannot identify its PAA/protocol; or
- the TMB prescriptive-delegation relationship has not been completed when required.

---

## Gate 5 — Controlled substances

Complete this gate before prescribing **any** controlled substance.

- [ ] The controlled substance is within PMHNP scope and my individual competence.
- [ ] The applicable PAA/facility authorization permits the schedule/category.
- [ ] I have my **own active DEA registration** appropriate to the practice; I am not using the physician's DEA registration as mine.
- [ ] EHR/pharmacy setup contains the required controlled-substance prescriber/delegating information.
- [ ] I understand the Schedule III-V 90-day/physician-consultation requirements.
- [ ] The clinic has a method to track/document required physician controlled-substance consultations.
- [ ] I know the additional consultation requirement for Schedule III-V prescribing in a child younger than two years.
- [ ] Texas PMP access is active and integrated into my workflow.
- [ ] I know when PMP lookup is mandatory and how to document the review and prescribing rationale.
- [ ] Electronic prescribing of controlled substances is configured, or a valid statutory exception/individual waiver applies.
- [ ] Any pain-management or acute-pain opioid limits relevant to the service line are operationalized.

**Detailed reference:** [`controlled-substances.md`](controlled-substances.md)

### Schedule II STOP check

Do **not** assume outpatient Schedule II authority simply because the medication is common in psychiatry.

- [ ] If Schedule II prescribing is contemplated, I have verified that the patient and practice setting fit one of Texas's narrow authorized hospital facility-based or qualified hospice pathways.
- [ ] If a Schedule II prescription is part of hospital discharge, the pharmacy/location requirement has been verified.

If these conditions are not satisfied, the PMHNP should not issue the Schedule II prescription under delegated APRN authority.

---

## Gate 6 — Texas PMP workflow

For opioids, benzodiazepines, barbiturates, and carisoprodol:

- [ ] PMP is checked before prescribing unless a current statutory exception applies.
- [ ] The PMP review is documented in the medical record.
- [ ] The rationale for the controlled-substance prescription is documented.
- [ ] If an exception is used (for example cancer, sickle cell disease, or hospice care), the required exception documentation is included.
- [ ] If an authorized staff member performs the query, I still review the information and remain responsible for the prescribing decision/documentation.

**Detailed reference:** [`controlled-substances.md`](controlled-substances.md)

---

## Gate 7 — Telemedicine / remote practice

Complete before remote patient care.

- [ ] Patient physical location is confirmed at each encounter when jurisdiction matters.
- [ ] I hold the APRN/telemedicine authority required in the jurisdiction where the patient is located.
- [ ] I am not relying on the RN Nurse Licensure Compact as an APRN multistate license.
- [ ] Texas telehealth/telemedicine informed consent is documented when the patient is in Texas.
- [ ] Consent records are retained under the current Texas retention rule.
- [ ] Assessment, diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, and documentation meet the same standard expected in person.
- [ ] Emergency escalation and local-resource procedures are available for the patient's location.
- [ ] Controlled-substance PMP, Schedule, DEA, and e-prescribing rules are applied the same way when triggered remotely.
- [ ] If controlled substances are prescribed without a prior in-person evaluation, current federal DEA telemedicine authority has been verified.

**Detailed reference:** [`telemedicine.md`](telemedicine.md)

### Federal expiration STOP check

The broad DEA/HHS temporary controlled-substance telemedicine flexibilities documented in this project run through **December 31, 2026**.

- [ ] If the encounter occurs after December 31, 2026—or close enough that a rule change may have occurred—I have rechecked current DEA/HHS rules before relying on this file.

---

## Gate 8 — Operational readiness

The legal documents only help if the clinical workflow can comply with them.

- [ ] EHR correctly displays my Texas APRN/prescriptive information.
- [ ] E-prescribing and DEA configuration have been validated before the first controlled-substance prescription.
- [ ] Delegating physician information is correct in the EHR/prescription workflow.
- [ ] PMP access works.
- [ ] Required chart review can be identified/tracked.
- [ ] Monthly QA meetings have a calendar/process and documentation location.
- [ ] Referral pathways exist for medical issues outside PMHNP scope.
- [ ] Emergency psychiatric escalation/involuntary-treatment procedures are understood for each care setting.
- [ ] Lab/diagnostic ordering permissions match the written delegation and employer privileges.
- [ ] Pharmacy, prior-authorization, and medication-administration workflows have been tested where relevant.

---

## Gate 9 — First-day verification packet

Keep a secure, current set of the following or know exactly where the employer maintains them:

- [ ] Texas RN privilege verification.
- [ ] Texas PMHNP/APRN license verification.
- [ ] National PMHNP certification verification.
- [ ] Prescriptive authority verification, if applicable.
- [ ] DEA registration, if applicable.
- [ ] Current written physician delegation protocol/practice agreement.
- [ ] Current PAA or facility-based protocol, if prescribing.
- [ ] TMB prescriptive-delegation registration confirmation, if applicable.
- [ ] Employer credentialing/privileging confirmation.
- [ ] Malpractice/professional liability confirmation.
- [ ] Current controlled-substance/e-prescribing waiver documentation, if one is being relied upon.

Do not commit these personal/licensure documents or identifiers into this research repository unless there is a specific secure need and the repository policy is revised to support that use.

---

## 60-second go/no-go screen

Before the first patient encounter, all six statements should be true:

- [ ] **Licensed:** My Texas PMHNP/APRN license is active for the role/population I will treat.
- [ ] **Competent/credentialed:** I am individually prepared and employer-authorized for the services.
- [ ] **Delegated:** The required physician delegation protocol covers the medical acts I will perform.
- [ ] **Authorized to prescribe:** If prescribing, my Texas prescriptive authority + PAA/facility protocol + TMB delegation setup are complete.
- [ ] **Controlled substances are separately cleared:** If applicable, DEA + schedule authority + PMP + e-prescribing requirements are operational.
- [ ] **Remote care is jurisdictionally cleared:** If telemedicine, I am authorized where the patient is located and current Texas/federal telemedicine rules are satisfied.

**If any answer is no or unknown, resolve it before proceeding rather than relying on an assumption.**
