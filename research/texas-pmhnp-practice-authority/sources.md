## TX-004 — Texas BON APRN Practice FAQ

- **Inspection status:** inspected for scope, delegation, prescriptive-authority, PAA, facility-based, and controlled-substance topics
- **Authority level:** official agency interpretive guidance
- **Citation / title:** APRN Practice FAQ
- **Issuing body:** Texas Board of Nursing
- **Source type:** regulator/agency FAQ
- **Official URL:** https://www.bon.texas.gov/faq_practice_aprn.asp.html
- **Date inspected:** 2026-09-03
- **Relevant question:** practical BON interpretation of Rules 221 and 222 and Chapter 157
- **Key verified uses:**
  - scope is founded on APRN educational preparation, role, and population focus;
  - physician delegation does not expand licensure scope;
  - delegated medical aspects of care require an appropriate written delegation mechanism;
  - a prescriptive authority agreement is used in applicable non-facility-based practice, while facility-based practice may use the authorized protocol pathway;
  - current PAAs require monthly quality-assurance meetings, with chart-review requirements determined by the parties within the statutory framework;
  - Schedule II prescribing is limited to specified hospital facility-based and hospice circumstances.
- **Verification status:** verified as current BON guidance; controlling statute/rule governs if wording differs

## Source-use rules for this project

1. A statute or adopted rule outranks an FAQ, bulletin, academic paper, or prior assignment.
2. BON/TMB FAQs are used to understand agency interpretation and implementation, not to replace the underlying law.
3. Prior Drive work may identify a question or citation but cannot verify current law.
4. Every time-sensitive legal/regulatory claim must carry the project's review date and be rechecked before external publication if material time has passed or a rule/statutory change is possible.
5. No source should be described as fully inspected unless the relevant material was actually reviewed.
